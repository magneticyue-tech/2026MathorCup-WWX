# AGENTS.md — Codex

Shared team standards for all agents live in `COLLABORATION.md`.
Read it, and `memory/MEMORY.md`, at the start of every task, and follow both.

Codex-specific notes:
- You do not get automatic file-change notifications or auto-loaded memory. At
  the start of every task re-read: the repository commit log (`git log` /
  `git diff`), `memory/MEMORY.md`, the updated `model/` living docs, the updated
  solver outputs under `figures/`, the updated derived data under
  `code/data/processed/`, and open threads in `review/` addressed to your team.
  See the cross-team synchronization section of `COLLABORATION.md`.
