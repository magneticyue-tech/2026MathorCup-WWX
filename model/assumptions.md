# Model assumptions

Maintained continuously. This file records only global assumptions — those
conditioning the data, scope, or environment of the whole problem. Each states
what is assumed, why it is reasonable, and its effect on the model. The paper's
assumptions section is generated from this file. Method-local assumptions
(functional-form, estimator, or scope choices tied to one method) are stated in
the relevant `q<N>_methodology.md` at the point of use, not listed here.
Reference every assumption by content, not by serial number.
